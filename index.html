At Better You, we look at the individual within the context of their family and environment, helping children and parents understand each other better. We also believe that supporting parents is an important part of supporting children.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consider counselling?
You don't need to wait until a problem becomes overwhelming. Counselling can be useful whenever you feel stuck, overwhelmed, confused, emotionally drained or unable to manage a situation on your own.
Is counselling confidential?
Counselling sessions are conducted with privacy and respect, subject to applicable professional and safety-related exceptions.
Do I need a diagnosis to see a counsellor?
No. People seek counselling for many different reasons, including relationship difficulties, stress, personal growth, parenting concerns, career decisions and emotional challenges.
Can parents seek counselling for their children?
Yes. Parents can discuss their concerns with a counsellor and explore appropriate support for their child.
How many sessions will I need?
There is no fixed number. The duration and frequency of counselling depend on the individual's concerns, goals and progress.
Are sessions available online?
Yes, online counselling sessions are available to ensure convenient and confidential support wherever you are located.
